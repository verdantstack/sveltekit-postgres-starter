# Variable: POLICY\_REVIEW\_NOTICE

> `const` **POLICY\_REVIEW\_NOTICE**: `"Sample configuration. Retention, erasure-SLA and transfer settings are a starting point for your counsel and subprocessor agreements, not legal advice."` = `'Sample configuration. Retention, erasure-SLA and transfer settings are a starting point for your counsel and subprocessor agreements, not legal advice.'`

The notice that must travel with every policy value.

## Remarks

The numbers below are a defensible starting configuration, not a
legal opinion. A starter kit that implied otherwise would be handing its
buyer a compliance claim they cannot support — the exact failure mode that
makes "SOC 2 ready" badges worthless. Ship them as a starting point and have
counsel set the real values.
